# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is Vlady Yakovenko, maintaining a durable personal archive of work over time. Public visitors can use the archive to understand the continuity between software, design, music, visuals, releases, and events.

## Product Purpose

The site documents Vlady's work, releases, writing, and ongoing practice in one public place. Success means the archive remains truthful, navigable, and useful as new milestones accumulate.

## Positioning

This is a first-person, cross-disciplinary record rather than a conventional portfolio funnel. It connects professional work, self-initiated software, music, visual practice, and public activity without flattening them into a single discipline.

## Operating Context

The site is a statically generated Astro portfolio and blog. Content is maintained in repository data, page-local collections, and Markdown posts, then published as a public website.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site supports light and dark themes and responsive layouts.
- Related project content intentionally appears on multiple surfaces with audience-specific copy.
- Timeline content must use verified repository material only. Missing dates or events must not be invented.
- Ongoing work must be labeled clearly.
- Internal links stay in the current tab; external links open in a new tab.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: polyterative / Vlady Yakovenko.
- Voice: plain, direct, first person, with no hype or corporate language.
- The archive spans design, code, sound, visuals, writing, and public work.
- Existing portrait, project imagery, app screenshots, music artwork, and favicon are the approved asset set.

## Evidence on Hand

- Professional experience and current work: `src/pages/about.astro`
- Applications and release facts: `src/data/apps.ts`
- Project archive and public talks: `src/pages/projects.astro`
- Music releases and artwork: `src/pages/music.astro`, `public/music/`
- Dated writing: `src/content/blog/`
- Project and app imagery: `public/projects/`, `public/apps/`, `public/blog/`

No testimonials, audience metrics, client claims, or unrecorded dates should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Preserve the record, not a polished mythology.
2. Show connections across disciplines without erasing their differences.
3. Prefer verified specifics over impressive-sounding claims.
4. Make the archive easy to extend as new milestones occur.
5. Keep the interface quiet enough for the work itself to lead.
