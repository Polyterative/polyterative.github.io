## Scope and mode

New `/timeline` surface in Experience mode, inside the established portfolio world.

## Audience and job

Vlady maintains a truthful personal archive; public visitors explore how software, professional work, music, writing, and live activity overlap over time.

## Content and constraints

Use verified repository dates only. Show ongoing work explicitly. Reuse existing project, release, and post links. Preserve the current theme system, type, voice, and internal/external link behavior.

## Direction

Archive stratigraphy: stacked year bands connected by one continuous blue trace. Categories remain one-color but gain distinct node shapes. A compact chronogram makes the entire span legible in the first viewport; filters change both the chart and the archive.

## Memorable moment

The overview reveals several disciplines running in parallel, then resolves into a single connected record as the visitor scrolls.

## Unresolved decisions
